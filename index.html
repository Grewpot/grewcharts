THE STORY

The candle is only the result.

A candlestick tells you where the market opened, where it closed, and how far it travelled. But between those numbers is a much larger story.

Trades are executed. Volume accumulates. Buyers and sellers compete. Liquidity appears and disappears around the market.

Traditional charts compress all of that activity into a familiar shape. GrewCharts is an experiment in opening that shape up and exploring what happened underneath.

Price shows the move. The activity helps tell the story behind it.

WHY EXPLORE?

Look beyond the direction.

A chart can tell you that price moved higher. It cannot always tell you everything that happened while it was moving.

Where did activity increase? Where was volume concentrated? Did the market move quickly through an area or spend time trading there? How did available liquidity change?

GrewCharts is built around exploring those questions — not replacing traditional charts, but adding another layer of visibility to the market.

CURRENT STAGE

Built for exploration. Still under construction.

GrewCharts is currently an experimental demo under active development. We are testing ideas, refining calculations, improving visualizations and learning from how the platform behaves in real-world conditions.

That means features can change, data can occasionally be delayed or incomplete, and the platform may contain bugs, inaccuracies or unexpected behaviour.

Please don't trade based on GrewCharts yet. The current version is intended for exploration, testing and learning. Always verify market information independently before making trading or investment decisions.
